# Physics Lecture: Newton's Second Law of Motion & Fundamental Dynamics

## 1. Newton's Second Law of Motion
* **Fundamental Principle**: The force applied to an object is directly equal to the mass of that object multiplied by its acceleration.
* **Mathematical Representation**: 
  $$\mathbf{F = ma}$$
  * $\mathbf{F}$ = Force
  * $\mathbf{m}$ = Mass
  * $\mathbf{a}$ = Acceleration
* **Practical Implication**: Mass directly affects the force required for acceleration. 
  * *Example*: Accelerating a heavy object (e.g., a car) requires significantly more force than accelerating a light object (e.g., a bicycle).
* **Vector Nature**: Force is a **vector quantity**, meaning it inherently possesses both **magnitude** and **direction**.

## 2. Frictional Force
* **Definition**: **Friction** is a contact force that acts in direct opposition to the direction of motion.
* **Notation**: Denoted by a lowercase **$f$**.
